Five signs the gap is already costing you.

None of these show up on an invoice. They show up in lost time, stalled projects, and decisions nobody wants to own.

01

Nobody owns the technology direction.

Decisions get made by whoever is closest to the problem. No strategy, no roadmap, no single person accountable for where it is all heading. Everyone owns a piece. Nobody owns the outcome.

02

The risk picture is invisible until something breaks.

Security feels like box ticking, not managed risk. Then the board or your insurer asks a harder question about cyber and compliance, and the honest answer is "I will find out."

03

Your MSP manages itself.

They handle the tickets and respond when things break. But nobody holds them to account, checks what you are paying for, or makes sure the work points somewhere sensible.

04

Projects stall, drift, and restart.

They kick off with good intentions, then lose momentum. Six months later you are looking at the same problem, often with the same vendor, and a budget that has quietly grown.

05

Head office and the sites run on different worlds.

Corporate systems on one side, the technology in each store, branch, or site on the other. Cameras, alarms, access control, point of sale, all of it. Both carry risk. Nobody governs the gap between them, and that gap is where the surprises hide.

If two or more of these sound familiar, the gap is costing you. It does not close itself.

Questions people ask

A few things worth knowing.

What does a first engagement look like?
No hard sell and no long proposal before you have seen any value. Week one is a focused conversation with you and whoever needs to be in the room. We learn how you are set up, where it hurts, and what good looks like. Month one, the quick wins are in motion, ownership is clear, and your vendors know who they are dealing with. Ongoing, what clients say most often around month three or four is "I do not think about IT anymore." Not because nothing happens, but because when it does, it gets handled.
Do you replace our existing IT provider?
It comes down to one question: is the provider worth keeping? If they are, we leave them in place and run the strategy above them. We own the direction and hold them to account, and they keep delivering. If they are the problem, we replace them and become your technology function ourselves. We migrate you off cleanly and own the result end to end. We will give you an honest read on which one you need, even when the answer is keep them. We are not a helpdesk or a ticket queue either way.
How does pricing work?
Running the strategy above your existing provider is a fixed monthly retainer. Replacing the provider and becoming your technology function is one monthly fee covering strategy, governance, security, vendor management, and delivery. One-off assessments and projects are scoped and priced before any work begins. We quote and work in day rates, not hours, so you are not second guessing time spent. For large projects or on-site work outside Auckland, the approach is agreed upfront. No surprises.
What size of business do you work with?
If you are big enough to feel real pain from technology that does not quite work, but not large enough to justify a full-time CIO, CISO, and internal IT team, you are likely a good fit. We typically work with New Zealand businesses in retail, manufacturing, food production, logistics, primary industry, construction, and distribution. Often multi-site or franchise operations running the same systems across many locations, often with connected devices and operational technology in the field, and usually spending meaningful money on technology without a senior person governing how it is used.
Do you work with boards and executives directly?
Yes, and it matters. Many businesses come to us because a board or CEO is asking harder questions about technology risk, cyber insurance, or why projects keep stalling. Part of the value is translating the technology picture into language that works at board level. Clear risk posture, honest progress, and a defensible investment story. If you need someone who can sit in a board meeting and hold the technology and security conversation, that is core to what we do.
What experience do you have with operational technology and connected devices?
Significant hands-on experience across New Zealand businesses where corporate IT meets the technology running in the field. That covers cameras, alarm panels, access control, and networked devices deployed across many sites, as well as manufacturing, food processing, and primary industry settings with SCADA, PLCs, and plant equipment. This technology carries a different risk profile to a standard office network. Uptime, safety, physical access, and legacy hardware all intersect. We bring the IT governance lens and the operational reality together, rather than forcing a standard IT framework onto an environment it does not fit.
What if we need on-site support outside Auckland?
We have a long-standing network of trusted partners across New Zealand, including for multi-site rollouts and in manufacturing and primary industry where on-site presence matters. For hands-on delivery outside Auckland, or across many sites at once, we bring in people we know well and have worked with before. You deal with us. We deal with them. The accountability stays in one place.
