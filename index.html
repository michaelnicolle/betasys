Questions people ask

A few things worth knowing.

What kind of businesses do you work with?
New Zealand businesses in retail, manufacturing, food production, logistics, primary industry, construction, and distribution. Often multi-site or franchise operations running the same systems across many locations, frequently with security and connected devices in the field. The common thread is a business big enough to feel real pain from technology that does not quite work, but not large enough to justify a full internal IT and security team.
Do you just fix things, or do you help the business grow?
Both, and the order matters. Managed IT keeps the day to day running so technology is one less worry. Transformation is where we move the business forward, designing and delivering the technology that helps you grow. We lead with outcomes, then back them with the dependable day to day support that makes them stick. We are not a break-fix shop waiting for the phone to ring.
Can you handle security across all our sites?
Yes. We treat physical and digital security as one picture. Cameras in the field, and the networks, data, and systems behind them. For a multi-site or franchise business, the value is in joining it all up. One clear view of what is protected and what is exposed across every location, managed and tested, not assumed.
What happens if we get hit by ransomware?
This is what the six layers are built for. Detection and response runs 24x7, so an attack is caught and contained fast, not discovered on Monday morning. Your backups are held offsite in immutable storage, which means they cannot be encrypted or deleted by an attacker, and your disaster recovery is planned and tested so we know it works. If the worst happens, we restore you and get the business trading again with as little disruption as possible. We also help you answer your cyber insurer honestly at renewal, backed by the controls already in place, so your cover holds when you need it.
We already have an IT provider. Where do you fit?
It depends on whether they are working for you. If they are, we can deliver specific projects or sit above them through myCIO to own the direction and hold them to account. If they are the problem, we can take it all on ourselves and become your one accountable partner. We will give you an honest read on which makes sense, even when the answer is to keep what you have.
Do you work outside Auckland?
Yes. We deliver across New Zealand, including multi-site rollouts where on-site presence matters. We have a long-standing network of trusted partners we know well and have worked with before. For work outside Auckland or across many sites at once, you deal with us and we deal with them. The accountability stays in one place.
What is myCIO, and do I need it?
myCIO is our senior technology and security leadership offer, for when the challenge is bigger than any one project. Think board-level technology questions, no one senior owning the direction, or a major investment you want an independent view on first. Most businesses start with BetaSys for delivery and support, then step up to myCIO when they need leadership that owns the whole outcome. You can read the full picture on the myCIO page.
